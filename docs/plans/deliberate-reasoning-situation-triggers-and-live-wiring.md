## §1 Summary / TL;DR

**One-line objective:** replace the deliberate-reasoning gate's conversation-keyword triggers with **situation/behavior signals derived from the structure of the pending action and recent behavioral history**, and wire the proven slice-1 gate→loop into **live primary-session turns** — default-OFF, firing only on a real structural signal, with the slice-1 zero-collateral property preserved as a live invariant.

**What ships in this slice:**
1. A new **situation-signal substrate** for the gate: deterministic detectors that classify the **pending tool call** and **recent tool-result history**, replacing `_HEDGE_RE`/`_STAKES_RE` keyword-scanning of the conversation. The slice-1 `GateSignals`/`evaluate_gate` shape is preserved; the *inputs and detectors* change.
2. A concrete **v1 situation set** (the signals that ship first), each with a deterministic detection definition that classifies the action, not the conversation (§3.2).
3. A **live-wiring path**: the gate fires inside a real primary-session turn via the EXISTING PreToolUse hook seam (about-to-act signals) and a pre-response surface (draft-level signals, see RF-3 for the honest gap), engaging the slice-1 re-think loop on a fire, under a single default-OFF switch.
4. The **default-OFF / no-collateral guarantee** carried forward as a live invariant: a turn with no structural signal is byte-identical to baseline; the gate fires on zero normal turns; enabling is one explicit reversible switch.

**AC families:** `AC.TRIG.{1..4}` (the situation-trigger substrate) + `AC.WIRE.{1..4}` (the live-wiring + no-collateral) + one outcome-altitude AC `AC.WIRE.OA` (a live end-to-end real-situation fire that changes the outcome, with zero firing on normal turns).

**Key decisions baked (recommendations = the proposed ruling; the owner rules from the §16 summary):**
- **D-SIT.1** — **Hybrid (option iii), structural-first.** Ship the structural/behavioral deterministic signals as the always-on cheap floor (option i); make the per-turn LLM self-assessment (option ii) a **deferred, explicitly-gated escalation** for ambiguous structural signals, NOT a v1 always-on cost. Recommendation + rationale §3.1.
- **D-SIT.2** — the **v1 situation set** is the four structural signals in §3.2, all calibrated to fire on the 2026-06-24 incident: (1) about-to-run-an-unbounded/expensive operation; (2) repeating-an-approach-that-just-failed-this-turn; (3) about-to-act-irreversibly-on-the-user's-machine; (4) high-blast-radius mutation. §3.2.
- **D-SIT.3** — the **structural-vs-keyword line** (the heart of the plan): a signal is admissible iff it is computed from the **pending action's structure** (tool name, tool arguments' structure, target path, recent tool-RESULT history) and NOT from the natural-language content of the user's prompt or the model's draft. §3.3.
- **D-SIT.4** — the **wiring mechanism** is the EXISTING PreToolUse hook seam for about-to-act signals (compose with the live `in_thread_work_budget_guard`/`wd_discipline_guard` pattern), plus a pre-response surface for draft-level signals. §3.4.
- **D-SIT.5** — **default-OFF + escalation policy**: default-OFF is preserved exactly as slice-1's `LOAM_DELIBERATE_REASONING`; the option-(ii) self-assessment escalation is a SECOND, independently-default-OFF switch (never on without the structural floor on). §3.5.
- **D-SIT.6** — **slice-2a relationship**: slice-2a's `SelfAttribution.matches()` prompt-keyword matching gets the **same treatment** — it is superseded by the situation-signal substrate as the gate's input contract; slice-2a's self-model is **folded in as a conditioning SOURCE of situation signals** (a recorded self-attribution becomes a structural signal weight), NOT a parallel keyword matcher. The self-model EXPERIMENT (the behavior-shaping measurement) is unaffected and stays paused at its own gate. §3.6.

**F2 RF on scope realism (see §10) — stated plainly up front:**
- **A deterministic gate cannot have "zero triggers."** It must fire on SOME signal; the redesign moves the signal from conversation-words to action-structure, it does not eliminate the signal. Anyone expecting "no triggers at all" is expecting the gate to not exist. The honest claim is "no *conversation-keyword* triggers" (§10 RF-1).
- **Option (ii) is too costly to run every turn on the subscription path.** The cheap form (token-level logprob self-assessment) is NOT exposed on `claude -p` / the subscription path; option (ii) therefore means an explicit extra model call per turn, which is the per-turn token cost slice-1's whole design (D-MGRL.1) ruled out. So (ii) is deferred to an escalation, never the v1 floor (§10 RF-2).
- **The structural signals cannot cleanly cover the draft-QUALITY cases.** The signals classify *actions* and *behavioral history*; a turn whose only problem is a subtly-wrong *answer* with no risky pending action and no repeated failure produces NO structural signal. That class is exactly what the old `LOW_CONFIDENCE` hedge-regex tried (badly) to catch. This slice does NOT fully replace that coverage — it names the gap honestly and routes it to the deferred option-(ii) escalation (§10 RF-3). This is the load-bearing honest limitation.
- **Trigger-redesign and live-wiring ARE separable slices** (Lens 5). They are bundled here because the live-wiring is what makes the redesigned triggers testable end-to-end (the outcome-altitude AC needs both), but §6 names a clean sub-seal boundary so the builder may ship them as two commits if the wiring proves heavier than estimated (§10 RF-4).

## §3 Halt-and-surface BEFORE build (recorded + ruled at plan-authoring)

### 3.1 — D-SIT.1 — the (i)/(ii)/(iii) fork: HYBRID, structural-first (THE CENTRAL DECISION)

**The option space (each evaluated honestly):**

- **(i) Structural/behavioral deterministic signals.** Classify the pending tool call + recent tool-result history. **Free** (no token cost), runs every turn, deterministic, sits naturally on the existing PreToolUse seam. **Coarse** — it catches risky/repeating *actions*, not subtly-wrong *answers* with no risky action (RF-3). Strongest fit for the motivating incident (which was entirely about a runaway action + a repeated failure, with no conversation-keyword signal).
- **(ii) Lightweight per-turn LLM self-assessment.** The model rates its own confidence/risk each turn. **Sharper** — it can catch the draft-quality case (i) misses. **But the cheap version (token-level logprobs) is NOT exposed on the subscription/`claude -p` path** (`feedback_no_anthropic_api_key` — we are subscription-only), so on our path (ii) means an **explicit extra `claude -p` call every turn** — exactly the per-turn token cost slice-1's D-MGRL.1 ruled out as the trap. Running it on EVERY turn is not viable on our path.
- **(iii) Hybrid.** Structural signals as the always-on cheap floor; escalate to a self-assessment **only** when a structural signal is ambiguous (or to cover the named draft-quality gap), so the costly model call is spent on a small fraction of turns, not all of them.

**Recommendation: (iii) Hybrid, structural-first — but ship ONLY the structural floor (i) in v1, with the (ii) escalation DESIGNED but DEFAULT-OFF and deferred to a follow-on enablement.** Rationale (per F4 scope↔confidence + Lens 5):
  1. **Confidence that the structural floor is correct is HIGH** — the existing PreToolUse guards prove the pattern works live, the motivating incident is fully covered by structural signals, and it is free. Tight scope: ship it.
  2. **Confidence that an always-on per-turn LLM self-assessment is worth its token cost is LOW** — slice-1 deliberately ruled out the LLM-per-turn gate (D-MGRL.1); reintroducing it as an always-on cost contradicts the proven design. Loose scope: design the seam, default it OFF, gate its enablement on a separate decision.
  3. **The hybrid is the honest answer to the draft-quality gap (RF-3)** without paying the always-on cost: the escalation is the route by which the draft-quality class (which the structural floor cannot cover) CAN be covered later, when/if the owner rules the per-turn cost worth it.

**What this means concretely for v1:** v1 ships the structural floor (i) as the live gate substrate. The (ii) escalation is a designed, named, default-OFF seam — built only far enough that enabling it is a single reversible switch (mirroring slice-1's pattern), not a re-architecture. The owner can enable it later without a new slice.

### 3.3 — D-SIT.3 — the structural-vs-keyword line (THE HEART OF THE PLAN)

**The line the owner will scrutinize, stated as a test:** a candidate signal is **admissible** iff its detection reads ONLY from:
  1. the pending action's **tool name** (e.g. Bash, Write, Edit, a search tool, a network tool);
  2. the **structure of the tool's arguments** — the regex/quantifier shape, the recursion/breadth flags, the presence/absence of a result bound, the target path, the target's size/type;
  3. the **recent tool-RESULT history** — action metadata (tool name, argument-shape hash, exit/result class: success/empty/failure/timeout) of the last N calls.

A candidate signal is **INADMISSIBLE** (this is what we are removing) iff its detection reads from:
  - the **natural-language content of the user's prompt** (the old `_STAKES_RE` over `prompt_text`);
  - the **natural-language content of the model's draft answer** (the old `_HEDGE_RE` over `draft_text`).

**The bright-line restatement:** "classify the pending action" = look at WHAT the tool call will DO and what recent calls DID (their structure + outcome). "Keyword-scan the prompt" = look at WHAT WORDS appear in the conversation. The first is admissible; the second is what this slice removes.

**The honest edge cases (named, not hidden):**
  - **`NOVELTY` (slice-1's third trigger) is borderline.** It is set-membership on a `task_class` label — not a conversation keyword-scan, so by the test above it is admissible AS A MECHANISM. But if the `task_class` label is itself *derived* by keyword-scanning the prompt upstream, the keyword scan has just moved one layer up. **Ruling:** `NOVELTY` MAY be retained in v1 ONLY IF the task-class label is derived structurally (e.g. from the tool-call sequence shape), NOT from a prompt keyword-scan; otherwise it is retired with the other keyword triggers. The builder verifies the label's derivation; if it is keyword-derived, `NOVELTY` is retired (halt-trigger 5).
  - **A regex over the tool ARGUMENT is still a regex** — but it reads the *action's structure* (the pending command's quantifier shape), not the *conversation's words*. This is admissible: detecting "this pending search uses an unbounded backtracking quantifier" is classifying the action, even though the detector itself is a pattern over the command string. The distinction is the SOURCE (the pending tool argument) not the TECHNIQUE (pattern-matching). The owner should confirm they accept this reading — it is the subtle but load-bearing distinction (see §16 surface).

### 3.4 — D-SIT.4 — the wiring mechanism (compose with the existing PreToolUse hooks)

**Ruling / recommendation:** the about-to-act signals fire through a **PreToolUse hook** that composes with the existing `in_thread_work_budget_guard.py` / `wd_discipline_guard.py` pattern — same envelope (`tool_name` + `tool_input`), same fail-open contract (any error → exit 0 / allow), same override sentinel, registered in `.claude/settings.json`. The hook is the harness-side adapter that reads the envelope, calls the `framework/deliberate-reasoning/` gate, and on a fire engages the re-think loop (surfacing the deliberate critique as a warn `systemMessage`, or blocking the action toward re-think on the high-severity signals). The draft-level signals fire through a pre-response surface (builder's call: a Stop/pre-response hook or the `process_turn` draft seam). **Rationale:** the live workspace ALREADY enforces structural PreToolUse classification with this exact pattern; a parallel mechanism duplicates it. The framework owns the DECISION (the gate), the hook owns the HARNESS ADAPTATION (reading the envelope, surfacing the result) — clean separation, mirrors how `intent_classifier` (framework) is wired by `intent_classifier_inbound.py` (hook). **Ordering note:** the deliberate-gate hook composes with the existing guards — if `wd_discipline_guard` or `in_thread_work_budget_guard` already BLOCKS the pending action (exit 2), that block stands; the deliberate gate is a sibling classifier, not an override. Ordering is deterministic per `settings.json` registration; the deliberate gate should run such that a hard block from a safety guard short-circuits it (no point re-thinking a blocked action). The builder fixes the order in `settings.json` and documents it. **Method-in-AC test passed:** "the gate fires inside a live primary-session turn on a structural signal and engages the loop" can be met by a PreToolUse hook, a Stop hook, or a combination — the wiring mechanism is the builder's call within the existing-pattern constraint.

### 3.5 — D-SIT.5 — default-OFF + escalation policy (the live no-collateral invariant)

**Ruling / recommendation:** default-OFF is preserved EXACTLY as slice-1's `LOAM_DELIBERATE_REASONING` reversible opt-in (AC.MGRL.7) — with the layer off, the gate is not consulted, no hook fires the deliberate path, and every turn is byte-identical to baseline. The **live invariant** carried forward from slice-1's `gain_on_unflagged == 0`: **the gate fires on ZERO normal turns** — a turn with no structural signal produces no deliberate-loop invocation, no warn/block, no perturbation. The option-(ii) self-assessment escalation gets a **SECOND, independently-default-OFF switch**: it can be enabled ONLY when the structural floor is already on, and even then it spends a model call only on the small fraction of turns where a structural signal is ambiguous (never every turn). **Rationale:** slice-1's reversible default-OFF is the proven property that kept the experiment's baseline genuinely unperturbed; live-wiring must not weaken it. The two-switch design keeps the free structural floor and the costly escalation independently controllable, so enabling the floor never silently turns on per-turn token spend. **Method-in-AC test passed:** "default-OFF, fires on zero normal turns, reversible, the escalation independently gated" is an outcome; the switch mechanism (env var, settings flag, sentinel) is the builder's call.

### 3.6 — D-SIT.6 — the slice-2a self-model relationship (RULING)

Folding the self-model into the structural signal layer preserves its value while removing the keyword scan. **Scope note:** slice-2a is uncommitted/paused; this ruling REDIRECTS its eventual integration seam (when it un-pauses, it conditions structural signals, not a keyword matcher) — it does not require touching slice-2a's paused code in THIS slice. If the builder finds slice-2a's keyword matcher is already live anywhere, that is halt-trigger 6. **Method-in-AC test passed:** the relationship ruling is a scope/integration decision, not an AC; it shapes how a FUTURE slice-2a integrates, and is recorded so that integration does not silently re-introduce the keyword scan.

## §10 F2 Ruthless Feedback (honest doubts; named design risks)

- **RF-1 (the framing correction the owner needs). A deterministic gate cannot have "zero triggers" — it fires on SOME signal by definition.** Evidence: `gate.py::evaluate_gate` is a function of `GateSignals`; a gate with no signal source is a gate that never fires, i.e. the layer not existing. The redesign moves the signal from conversation-words (`_HEDGE_RE`/`_STAKES_RE` over `draft_text`/`prompt_text`) to action-structure (the PreToolUse envelope + result ring) — it does not remove the signal. **The honest claim is "no CONVERSATION-KEYWORD triggers," not "no triggers."** Alternative if the owner truly wants zero deterministic triggers: that is option (ii) (an LLM rates every turn) with NO structural floor — which is the per-turn-token-cost trap slice-1 ruled out, and is NOT recommended (RF-2). Naming this so the owner's objection is satisfied at the right altitude: we are removing keyword-on-conversation, keeping deterministic-on-action.
- **RF-2 (the cost wall on option ii). The cheap form of per-turn self-assessment is not available on our path.** Evidence: token-level logprob self-confidence is not exposed on `claude -p` / the subscription path (`feedback_no_anthropic_api_key` — we are subscription-only). So option (ii) on our path is an explicit extra `claude -p` call PER TURN — the exact per-turn token cost slice-1's D-MGRL.1 deliberately ruled out. **Therefore option (ii) is too costly to run every turn and is deferred to an ambiguous-signal-only escalation, default-OFF.** If the owner wants (ii) always-on anyway, the cost (a full extra model call every single turn) must be accepted explicitly — I recommend against it.
- **RF-3 (the load-bearing honest gap). The structural signals cannot cleanly cover the draft-QUALITY cases.** Evidence: the signals classify ACTIONS and behavioral HISTORY; a turn whose only flaw is a subtly-wrong ANSWER with no risky pending action and no repeated failure produces NO structural signal. That is precisely the class the old `LOW_CONFIDENCE` hedge-regex aimed at (and caught badly — hedge-words are a weak proxy for wrongness). **This slice does NOT fully replace that coverage.** The honest move: name the gap, ship the structural floor that DOES cover the high-impact action-runaway class (the motivating incident), and route the draft-quality class to the deferred option-(ii) escalation. Alternative considered + rejected: keep the hedge-regex as a draft-quality fallback — rejected because it IS the conversation-keyword trigger the owner objected to; relabeling it does not fix it. The owner should understand v1 trades draft-quality coverage (weakly served before) for clean action-runaway coverage (unserved before, and the actual incident).
- **RF-4 (separability). Trigger-redesign and live-wiring are separable slices (Lens 5).** Evidence: `AC.TRIG.*` is testable purely at the gate's unit level (no live turn needed); `AC.WIRE.*` is the live path. They are bundled because the outcome-altitude AC needs both (a live fire on a real structural situation). If the live-wiring proves heavier than the ~structural-floor estimate, §6 names a clean sub-seal: ship `AC.TRIG.*` first (the trigger substrate, immediately useful to the slice-1 experiment + any caller of the gate), then `AC.WIRE.*`. Recommendation: keep bundled (the wiring is the point — a redesigned trigger that never fires live is half a deliverable), but the builder may split on cost.
- **RF-5 (the structural-vs-keyword line has a subtle edge the owner must rule on). A pattern over the tool ARGUMENT is still a pattern-match.** Evidence: SIT.UNBOUNDED_OP detects an unbounded quantifier by pattern-matching the pending command string. Someone could argue "that's still a regex over text." The distinction (D-SIT.3): the SOURCE is the pending ACTION'S argument (what the command will DO), not the CONVERSATION (what the user said). I judge this admissible and the right line — but it IS the subtle call the owner objected around, so §16 surfaces it explicitly for the owner to confirm the reading. If the owner rejects even argument-pattern-matching, the structural floor shrinks to pure tool-type + path + result-history classification (still covers SIT.MACHINE_IRREVERSIBLE, SIT.REPEAT_FAILED, SIT.HIGH_BLAST_RADIUS; weakens SIT.UNBOUNDED_OP to "spawn/large-target without a bound" by metadata only).
